Mintae Kim

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mechanical Engineering at UC Berkeley, affiliated with Hybrid Robotics Lab and Berkeley AI Research (BAIR).

My research spans robotics, reinforcement learning, world models, VLA, and controls. I currently work across the humanoid loco-manipulation learning stack, from motion tracking and whole-body control to trajectory generation, world action models, and VLA post-training.

Email / CV / Scholar / LinkedIn / GitHub

Research

I study robot learning. My work connects low-level motion tracking and whole-body control with data and simulation pipelines, trajectory generation, world action models, model-based planning, and VLA post-training. Across these layers, I use RL to optimize high-performance low-level behavior and offline or goal-conditioned learning for high-level post-training.
