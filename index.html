Mintae Kim

I am a PhD student in Robotics at UC Berkeley, working at the intersection of reinforcement learning, stochastic optimal control, and robust decision-making under uncertainty. I am broadly interested in building learning-based control and decision systems that remain reliable under distribution shift and imperfect models.

My current interests include offline-to-online RL, robust / pessimistic learning, distributional robustness (e.g., Wasserstein-style ambiguity), and connecting control-theoretic structure to modern RL algorithms. I also explore quantitative research directions where these tools translate to sequential decision-making in markets.

Research

I work on reinforcement learning and control with an emphasis on robustness, uncertainty-aware decision-making, and theoretical structure. A recurring theme is designing learning objectives and operators that remain stable and performant when dynamics models are imperfect and data is limited.
